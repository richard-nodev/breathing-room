# Code of Conduct

Breathing Room is for people who smoke and are trying to notice it. Many of them are tired of being judged. The project should feel the same way the app does: warm, honest and free of judgment.

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or whether and how much they smoke.

## Please do

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Talk about the work, not the person.
- Accept that people use this app in different ways, and for different reasons.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Please don't

- Lecture, shame or moralize at anyone about smoking, here or in the app.
- Harass, insult or make personal or political attacks.
- Make sexualized comments or advances.
- Share anyone's private information without their permission. That includes their smoking data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately on GitHub, for example through the contact on their profile. Every report is handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
